# Trail notes

We started at the lower ridge trailhead around 7am, before the heat set in. The first mile climbs steadily through pine, then flattens out along a shoulder with views back toward the valley.

## Gear

- Two liters of water minimum. A third if it's forecast over 85 degrees.
- Trekking poles for the descent
- A light jacket for the summit wind

At the saddle the trail splits. The left fork is shorter but exposed the whole way. The right fork adds forty minutes but stays in tree cover until the final scramble.

> Take the right fork if it's already past 9am. The exposed section gets brutal in direct sun.

## Summit

The last quarter mile is a hands-on-rock scramble, not technical but slow going. Worth it for the ridge line view in both directions. Plan on six hours round trip for a relaxed pace.
